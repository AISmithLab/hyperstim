Where the classifiers sit

Every stage runs before the download. The two screens (metadata, then thumbnail) are trained against random pairs and only rule pairs out. The look-alike model is trained on what survives the screens, where most pairs are series episodes, same-song versions and shared footage; it orders those pairs for the agent and sets how far down the agent reads. The agent's label decides the download. After the download nothing changes: the overlap step and the full report confirm each pair.

No stage could send pairs straight to download. Among the top 50 pairs of the look-alike model, about 3 in 4 are re-skins; the rest are re-uploads and look-alikes, which the images alone can't tell apart. So the agent stays the last check before a download.

What each tier keeps

Pairs kept at a given share of same-length re-skins, per tier and model (5-fold cross-validation, with videos linked by a re-skin or re-upload kept in one fold). Logistic regression ranks smoothly; boosted trees rule out more at 98% but drop to keeping nearly everything when the last few re-skins look like random pairs.

Re-skins the screens lose

Most are medleys: a short song re-skinned inside a longer video of several songs. Duration and title don't match by design, and the thumbnail shows another song. They are a different strategy, and a screen built on the same-length re-skins misses them. Finding them needs a test of "contains", such as frames of the short video matched against many frames of the long one, which costs more images per video.

Recall audit

Pairs the cascade or the seed search would drop, labelled by the agent from the saved images (thumbnail and middle frame; gate frames only where they had been fetched), with the samples mixed on each sheet. The first four samples are the pairs closest to a cut-off, not a random draw, so their counts are a floor for that zone, not a rate. Only the random sample gives a rate: 0 of 100, so at most 3.6% of ordinary pairs (95% interval), which says little at this size.

Recommendation for the next step

  1. Screen with metadata and one thumbnail per video. The metadata screen (API only) rules out about 98% of pairs, and the thumbnail cuts the rest by more than half. Within one studio the metadata doesn't save thumbnails: nearly every video has a look-alike sibling, so every video needs its thumbnail. Fetch the other frames only for videos still in a pair after the thumbnail screen.
  2. Don't count on the ¼ and ¾ frames. On the pairs that have them, the boosted look-alike model's list barely changes with them (2,165 against 2,281 pairs at 95% recall) and gets longer at 98% (table above). An earlier fit, before the screens were fixed, showed a large gain that didn't hold up. They help the linear model, not the best one. Fetch them only where the agent needs them to decide a pair.
  3. Let the look-alike model set the agent's reading order, and have the agent read a few hundred pairs past the cut-off in each group. That doubles as the audit: 5 of the 150 pairs just below the cut were re-skins the seed search had missed too.
  4. Download only what the agent confirms. That is about 3% of videos here, against 9% in the seed search, which also downloaded re-uploads.
  5. Give medleys their own route. 14 of the 165 re-skins, and 4 of the 9 the audit found, are a song re-skinned inside a longer video. They fail the duration and title signals by design. A cheap way in: pairs where a short video's thumbnail or middle frame matches any frame of a long video with shared title words, sent to the agent as their own sheets.
  6. Expect about 90–95% recall, not 99%. The last few re-skins look like ordinary look-alikes: the agent's list is 1,701 pairs at 90%, 4,983 at 95% and 32,029 at 97%. At 95% the agent reads nearly as many sheets as the seed search did (499 against 583); the saving is in downloads, and at 90% in sheets too (171). Check the cut-offs on a second owner group before trusting them there, and rerun on the corrected labels.

Limits

  • All labels come from one studio. Cut-offs fitted here are a starting point, checked on a second owner group before they set its cut-offs.
  • Recall is among re-skins the seed search could find. The audit estimates the rest.
  • Some labels are being corrected in another session; this runs again on the corrected labels.
  • No GPU was needed: features for all pairs take under a minute, every model trains in a few minutes on the CPU within a 10 GB memory cap. Image requests set the time, not compute.

Re-skin Search

Kids TV (A) and Kids Channel (B), one company's two channels: which of their videos are the same animation re-skinned (new colors, backgrounds or characters over the same timing), and how was it changed? Every pair of their videos goes through a chain of filters, cheapest first; the agent labels what passes. Re-skins are downloaded and count only when their main content overlaps; their groups have their own page.

Channel story

Was Kids Channel built from Kids TV's catalog, or to test versions against each other? Catalog reuse would show as a burst of re-skins of old Kids TV videos soon after 2014-10-30; testing as repeated releases of two versions close together. Only confident re-skins (agent confidence 70% or more) whose main content overlaps count here.
